Welcome to Not a Website!

If you've stumbled upon this, congratulations! 🎉 You're in a place that officially denies its own existence. This isn't a blog, it's not a webshop, and it's definitely not a serious business. This is... something else. If you insist, there's a button underneath that you shouldn't click.

The internet is packed with useful websites: cooking blogs, news portals, online shops, productivity hacks… and yes, way too many cat memes. But we figured, why not go in the opposite direction? So here it is: Not a Website.
There’s nothing here you need. No products, no life-changing tips, no “10 ways to improve your morning routine.” Just… nothing. Well, okay, maybe a few buttons you really shouldn’t press. (Go on, press them anyway. We dare you.)
Think of this place as an experiment in pointless web design. A digital vacation from meaning. If you’ve ever wanted to waste time in the most unproductive way possible—congratulations, you’ve made it. ✅
